# Vision and scope

Place is a provider-neutral personal place workspace. A person can find, save, classify, visit,
rate, write about, import, reconcile, and selectively share places without making a provider's
identifier the canonical identity.

Initial scope covers the personal library, provider evidence, reviewable imports, search, visits,
writing, sharing controls, and administration. Future cross-user recommendations and AI Tools use
versioned Place contracts. They are not active Stage 1 flows.

Place does not own central login, public ingress, global family composition, or another product's
data. It does not treat provider observations as trusted canonical overwrite commands.
