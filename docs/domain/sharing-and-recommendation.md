# Sharing and recommendation

Sharing publishes an authorization-filtered projection of selected maps, collections, Places, and
writing. Viewing another user's map never grants access to private fields. Copying creates a new
library relationship with provenance; it does not share mutable ownership.

Public and unlisted use the same minimal projection shape. Public may become discoverable in a later
stage; unlisted requires possession of its opaque publication ID. Private, unknown, and malformed
publication identifiers all avoid disclosing member identity or private-field existence.

Future recommendations consume privacy-reviewed projections and return explanations or candidates
through a versioned interface. Recommendation infrastructure is not active in Stage 1.
